YOUR ATTENTION IS WORTH SOMETHING, TOO

Why spend your evening
switching between agents?

Give each agent a clear project and room to work. Make overnight runs part of your plans, and save your attention for the decisions that need you.

Set the destination

Describe the result you want, share the materials, and agree on what your agent can do. Give it a project to pursue.

Give it the night

Choose work your agent can carry forward with the instructions and permissions you’ve given it. Keep your agent running while you step away.

Come back to the work

Review progress when you return. Make the calls that need your judgment, then give the next stage its direction.

A FEW PRACTICAL ANSWERS

Before you begin.

Do I need to switch agents?

Kaiiron currently works with Codex, OpenCode, and Pi. You keep using your agent to give instructions and review the work. Claude Code is not yet supported.

Will everything be finished in the morning?

Some projects may need more than a night. Timing depends on the AI service and the steps involved. Your agent may still need your input or approval. Keep it running; closing it can interrupt the work.

What does it cost?

Kaiiron is free to use. You pay the AI service separately for the work your agent does; a ChatGPT subscription does not cover that usage. Savings depend on the task and the service you use.

Will I need help setting it up?

Kaiiron is an early release, and installation currently requires technical experience. The setup guide is written for you or someone helping you get connected.
