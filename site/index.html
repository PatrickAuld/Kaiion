A FEW PRACTICAL ANSWERS

Before you begin.

Do I need to switch agents?

Kaiiron currently works with Codex, OpenCode, and Pi. You keep using your agent to give instructions and review the work. Claude Code is not yet supported.

How long will the work take?

This is best for work without a tight deadline. Tasks may take hours or days, depending on the AI service and how many steps are involved. Keep your agent running; closing it can interrupt the task.

What does it cost?

Kaiiron is free to use. You pay the AI service separately for the work your agent does; a ChatGPT subscription does not cover that usage. Savings depend on the task and the service you use.

Will I need help setting it up?

Kaiiron is an early release, and installation currently requires technical experience. The setup guide is written for you or someone helping you get connected.
